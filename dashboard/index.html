Definitions
Bid/ask exit
Closing value if sold at the bid and bought back at the ask, plus fees. The conservative mark used everywhere on this page.
Return on risk
P&L divided by the trade's maximum loss.
Profit factor
Sum of winning trades divided by the absolute sum of losing trades.
Max drawdown
Largest fall in total P&L from a previous high, measured from a starting value of zero.
GEX
Gamma x open interest x 100 x spot squared x 1%, in dollars of delta change per 1% move.
Wall
A strike with one of the three largest call (or put) gamma exposures in the run.
