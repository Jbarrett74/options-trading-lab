# Directional arm review, 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-24 (paper only)

Bottom line: the directional arm does not justify real money. 9 trades is far too small a sample, and 7 of the 9 were bearish spreads opened during a falling market, so the result cannot be told apart from "bet on the trend that happened" [Likely].

## Results (conservative fills: pay the ask, receive the bid, plus fees) [Certain]
- Trades: 9 closed, none open. 1 bullish (IWM call spread), 8 bearish (put spreads on SPY x3, QQQ x3, IWM x2).
- Wins / losses: 7 / 2 (77.8%).
- Total P&L: +$90.64. Average: +$10.07 per trade. Average max loss risked: $90.66.
- Best: +$49.84 (SPY 758/756, 11/13). Worst: -$78.08 (IWM 284/287 call spread, stop hit 11/12).
- The second loss: -$42.16 (IWM 265/263 put spread, time exit 11/24 after the rally).
- Exits: 5 time exits, 2 take profits, 1 stop, 1 time exit at a loss. The take profits gave up about $42-52 each versus mid because legs were $0.40-0.50 wide in the selloff.
- Bullish trades: 0 for 1. Bearish trades: 7 for 8, +$168.72.

## Context that explains most of the result [Certain]
- SPY fell from about 757.7 (11/11) to about 715.8 (11/24 close), about -5.5%, with a low near 689.8 on 11/20.
- The arm was bearish in a falling market. A rule of "buy a near-the-money put spread every day" would likely have won too [Likely]; that baseline was not run on paper, so this is not tested.

## Candidates rejected (17 no-trade rows)
- Most common reason: the time and event rules (8 rows). An expiry close enough to fit the "close by 1:15pm the day before expiry" rule was too near, and later expiries crossed NVDA earnings (11/17) or Wednesday's PCE/GDP (check d).
- Check (a), no agreement among gamma, trend and overseas: 3 rows (11/11).
- Cancel lines hit at the open: 3 rows (11/18-11/19).
- Discretionary gating by the briefing: 3-4 rows (11/19-11/20). The skipped IWM 269/267 on 11/19 would have won as IWM fell to 257 the next day [Likely]. Discretion cost at least one winner.

## Gamma walls and flip [Uncertain]
- Not tracked systematically; judged from run notes.
- Held as resistance: SPY 760 wall on 11/11 (pinned); SPY 740 call wall on the morning of 11/19.
- Broke: IWM put walls 281/282 (11/12); SPY put walls 730/725/720 (11/19 afternoon); SPY 710/700 and IWM 264-266 (11/20); SPY 720 call wall broken upward (11/23).
- Walls broke more often than they held in this trending, negative-gamma period. Negative gamma readings did line up with large moves (11/12-11/20).
- Robinhood premarket greeks were skewed on 11/19, 11/20 and 11/23; the recomputed (ALT) map was used.

## Asia / London vs direction [Uncertain]
- Where overseas markets leaned one way at entry (6 trades), the trade direction matched the outcome in 4. 11/11 (overseas up, bullish trade) lost. 11/20 (3 of 3 down, bearish IWM) lost after the 11/23 rally.
- 3 trades on 11/13 had mixed overseas signals and all won.
- 6 data points cannot separate signal from noise.

## Compared with docs/findings.md
- The backtests found 0% of small debit-spread configurations positive out of sample, and no entry rule beat a random-direction baseline. The paper result (+$90.64 on 9 trades) does not contradict that. It is small, in one market regime, with no baseline comparison [Certain].
- The cost problem the backtests flagged showed up live: crossing $0.40-0.50 bid/ask on each leg cut the two take profits by about half.

## Verdict
- Does not justify real money [Likely]. A real test needs 100+ trades across up, down and flat markets, a same-period random or "always bearish" baseline, and P&L at both mid and conservative fills.
- If the arm continues on paper: log the gamma-wall outcome as a field each day, keep discretionary overrides out (or log them as a separate arm), and record a daily baseline spread.

The credit arm continues to 2026-12-04 and is not reviewed here.
